# GRID - Business Summary
# Date: 2026-10-22

## What GRID Is
Full-stack social and professional network for live music.
Connects Artists, Promoters, Venues, and Fans.
Monetizes through memberships, ticketing, bookings, merch.

## Problem
- Artists: fragmented bookings, no recurring income, no fan ownership
- Promoters: manual ops, no customer data, payment risk
- Venues: empty weeknights, no loyalty, disconnected from talent
- Fans: broken discovery, no social layer, no perks

## Solution - 4 Sides
- Artists: profile, booking inbox, memberships, analytics
- Promoters: event creation, artist booking, memberships, CRM
- Venues: profile, memberships, reservations, analytics
- Fans: discovery, tickets, memberships, social layer, wallet

## Revenue Streams (Take Rates)
1. Club memberships: 15-30%
2. Ticket sales: 10-15%
3. Artist bookings: 10%
4. Merch: 15%
5. Promoted listings: flat fee

## Unit Economics - Superfan
Fan subscribes to:
- Fabric membership: £35/mo -> GRID gets £8.75
- DJ Proxy: £5/mo -> GRID gets £0.75
- Nightshift: £15/mo -> GRID gets £3.00
Total GRID revenue: £12.50/month per superfan
50K superfans = £7.5M/year

## Market
- UK live music: £6B
- Global TAM: £430B
- UK SAM: £500M
- Year 1-3 SOM: £15-25M

## Competitive Advantage
Only platform combining:
- Discovery + Ticketing (like Dice)
- Memberships (like Patreon)
- Artist booking (B2B SaaS)
- Social layer (like Instagram)

## Go-to-Market
Phase 1: Seed London (20 venues, 500 artists, 5K fans)
Phase 2: UK expansion (50 venues, 20K fans)
Phase 3: EU expansion (200 venues, 100K fans)
Phase 4: Global (1M+ fans)

## Financial Projections

Year 3: £20M revenue

## Growth Loop
Artist joins -> Posts event -> Promoter finds -> Venue books
-> Event live -> Fans buy -> Fans join memberships
-> Recurring revenue -> Artist earns more -> Stays
-> Attracts more artists (flywheel)
